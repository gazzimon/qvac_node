08 · Market

Two sides, one protocol.

Demand

Teams that need a second source

Companies whose operation runs on a single AI provider and cannot afford to be tied to one set of terms. They point an OpenAI client they already have at a different base URL and get continuity.

pulllower bill, no single vendor
Supply

Operators with hardware to monetise

Crypto node operators, server owners, and buyers acquiring machines specifically to earn from them. They already run infrastructure for yield; this is the same motion with one binary instead of a stack.

setupone command, no ops
pullidle compute becomes an asset
Where we actually are

Both sides are in validation. The protocol is verified across machines; what we have not measured is appetite with a live economic layer — which is exactly what E0–E3 unlocks.

The risk we are not hiding

Cold start, on both sides at once. Without providers there is no service; without demand there is no reason to add hardware. It is a market problem, not a technical one.
