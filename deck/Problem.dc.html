01 · The problem

Using AI means sending your prompt to someone else's machine.

Three companies set the price of inference, capture the margin on compute that millions already own, and can change the rules whenever they choose.

Every request is an act of faith. You cannot verify what happens to your prompt, and there is no second source the day the terms move.

Meanwhile the hardware to serve that inference already exists — in offices, in homes, in datacenters with spare capacity — idle most of the day.

The shape of the market today
every client→ one API
every prompt→ one datacenter
every margin→ one balance sheet
your leverage→ none

Encryption doesn't fix this. The provider has to read your prompt to answer it, and has to see your account to bill it.
