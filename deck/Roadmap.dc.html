07 · The plan

From a protocol that runs to a market that settles.

Ordered by dependency, not by how good it looks. Nothing downstream of metering can be built before metering exists — so that goes first, and it goes first whether or not we get into the program.

E0 metering A unit of account recomputable from the transcript. Everything else is blocked on this. 2 wks
E1 signed receipts Both sides sign what was consumed. Verifiable accounting with no chain and no enclave — it ships standalone. 2 wks
E2 hardening Persistent keys with scopes, load-based routing, failover, rate limits. You cannot bill against credentials that vanish on restart. 2 wks
E3 payments Deposit plus batched settlement, with x402 at the gateway edge for callers with no credit. 3–4 wks
E4 quality + reputation Entry benchmark, random spot-checks against known fingerprints, score derived from receipts. Lying becomes detectable and punishable. 3 wks
E5 the enclave Aggregation, settlement and reputation move inside. Matching designed with your team. program

E0 and E1 do not depend on the program. If we are not selected we still build them — we just carry on being a party the network has to trust.
