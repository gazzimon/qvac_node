06 · Where confidential execution fits

Move that piece into the enclave. Then we can't look.

What goes inside
metering

Consumption recomputed from the transcript, not asserted by the provider — who can otherwise inflate it 1,469% undetected (arXiv:2605.30040). If the enclave can't recompute it, it can only believe someone.

settlement

Receipts aggregated per provider and settled onchain in batches. Per-request payment is not viable at inference latency; batching is the honest design, not a shortcut.

reputation

Scored from signed receipts and random spot-checks, never from what a node says about itself.

All three are deterministic, asynchronous workloads — the WASM-job-with-onchain-settlement shape.

The open question we bring, not a promise
matching

It is where privacy is won or lost, but it also sits on the latency critical path. A round trip to the enclave per request may simply not be viable.

Our candidate design

An ephemeral key per request, so the serving node never sees a stable client identity and cannot build a profile across requests. Only the enclave can link that ephemeral key back to an account for billing. Matching stays off the critical path; the part that reveals linkage moves in.

This is the kind of problem where six weeks with your team is worth more than six months on our own.

With the enclave code published, this stops being a promise and becomes something anyone can check.
