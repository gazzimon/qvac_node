Where the case breaks

It needs a real bill to displace. The maths holds for anyone already spending $75+/month on AI. Below that, payback passes three years and the case dies.

It excludes network revenue entirely. That layer is not built, so it is not counted. Any earnings from serving others are upside we have not quantified.
