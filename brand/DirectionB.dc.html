Three pips

Pear seeds, radiating from an empty centre. It carries a double meaning that is exact rather than clever: a pip is what a pear distributes itself with, and pear seed is a command in your own package. Three is the smallest number that reads as a network rather than a pair. One is amber: not every peer is selling.
