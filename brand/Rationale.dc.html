The three images available
the fruit

Says where you come from. Instantly legible, and the only one that makes the name explain itself.

the seed

Says how you spread. One pip carries a whole tree — which is precisely what a P2P binary does.

the empty centre

Says what you refuse to be. The hardest to draw, and the one your whole pitch rests on.

A mark can hold two of these, never three. A and B keep the pear and give up the empty centre; C keeps the empty centre and gives up the pear. Which pair you want is the actual decision — not which drawing you like.
